Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the YouTube Shorts Publisher project uses Google services.

Information and access

With the account owner's authorization, the project accesses video files in a designated Google Drive folder and uploads selected videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

Use of information

Accessed files are used only for the publishing tasks configured by the account owner. The project does not sell personal information.

Data storage

Google OAuth credentials are used to authenticate requests. The project owner is responsible for protecting credentials and access tokens.

Third-party services

Google Drive and YouTube are subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.
